Questions owners ask before they reach out

What does HOD actually do?

We bring the right customers in with brand, website, SEO, AEO and ads, then fix what happens after: replies, follow-up and systems. We always start by finding the one bottleneck holding your business back.

Is the diagnostic really free? What’s the catch?

There’s no catch. The diagnostic is how we decide whether we can actually help you. If we can, we say so and scope a project. If we can’t, you still keep the findings.

How is HOD different from an agency or a freelancer?

An agency sells you a service and a freelancer does the task you hand over. HOD starts by finding which problem is really costing you customers, fixes that first across marketing and systems, and keeps one lead accountable for the result.

Can I hire you for just one thing?

Yes. If you already know you need a website, a CRM or an automation, send a project enquiry. We’ll check it solves the real problem first.

What does it cost?

The diagnostic is free. Projects are scoped after we understand the problem, so you never pay for guesses. We don’t publish prices until a project is scoped.

What happens after I send my enquiry?

A real person replies on WhatsApp and asks a few questions about how your business runs. Then we tell you plainly what we’d fix first, or that we’re not the right fit. No pitch deck, no pressure.
